## 1. Foundational Quantitative & Analytical Chemistry (CHE 101)

![CHE 101: Chemistry – 1 Laboratory Workflow](CHE101%20-%20Chemistry%20-%201%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Experiment 1: Analytical Balance, Law of Definite Proportions & Avogadro's Number
* **Law of Definite Proportions:** Verified fixed elemental mass ratios via thermal decomposition of $\text{KClO}_3 \rightarrow 2\text{KCl} + 3\text{O}_2\uparrow$ ($\approx 39.17\%$ oxygen mass loss).
* **Avogadro's Number ($N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}$):** Estimated via stearic acid monolayer surface area/thickness ratio and electrolytic copper deposition ($N_A = \frac{I \cdot t \cdot M_{\text{Cu}}}{n \cdot e \cdot \Delta m}$).

### Experiments 2 & 3: Standardization of $\text{HCl}$ & Acid–Base Volumetric Titration
* **Protocol:** Standardized secondary-standard $\text{HCl}$ against primary standard anhydrous $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ ($\pm 0.0001\ \text{g}$, methyl orange indicator) to concordant readings ($\pm 0.05\ \text{mL}$), followed by titration of unknown $\text{NaOH}$ (phenolphthalein indicator):
  $$M_{\text{HCl}} = \frac{2 \times \text{Mass of } \text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3\ (\text{g})}{105.99\ \text{g/mol} \times V_{\text{HCl}}\ (\text{L})} \qquad \text{and} \qquad M_{\text{NaOH}} \cdot V_{\text{NaOH}} = M_{\text{HCl}} \cdot V_{\text{HCl}}$$

### Experiment 4: Density Determination
* Determined intensive density ($\rho = m/V$) of liquids via calibrated pycnometer and irregular solids via water displacement.

## 2. Biophysical Chemistry: Calorimetry & Second-Order Reaction Kinetics (CHE 201)

![CHE 201: Biophysical Chemistry Laboratory Workflow](CHE201%20-%20Biophysical%20Chemistry%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Experiments 1, 2 & 3: Calorimetric Enthalpy Measurements
* **Calorimeter Constant ($C_{\text{cal}}$):** Determined by mixing known masses of hot and cold water ($c_w = 4.184\ \text{J/g}^\circ\text{C}$):
  $$C_{\text{cal}} = \frac{-(m_{\text{hot}} c_w \Delta T_{\text{hot}} + m_{\text{cold}} c_w \Delta T_{\text{cold}})}{\Delta T_{\text{cold}}}$$

| Experiment | Governing Thermodynamic Equation | Expected Standard Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heat of Fusion of Ice (Exp 2A)** | $m_{\text{ice}} \Delta H_{\text{fus}} + m_{\text{ice}} c_w (T_f - 0) + (m_w c_w + C_{\text{cal}})(T_f - T_i) = 0$ | $\Delta H_{\text{fus}} \approx \mathbf{+334\ \text{J/g}}$ ($+6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}$) |
| **Specific Heat of Unknown Metal (Exp 2B)** | $m_{\text{metal}} c_{\text{metal}} (T_f - T_{\text{metal}}) + (m_w c_w + C_{\text{cal}})(T_f - T_i) = 0$ | Identifies metal ($\text{Cu} \approx 0.385$, $\text{Al} \approx 0.897\ \text{J/g}^\circ\text{C}$) |
| **Heat of Solution (Exp 3A)** | $\Delta H_{\text{soln}} = \frac{-(m_{\text{soln}} c_{\text{soln}} + C_{\text{cal}})\Delta T}{n_{\text{solute}}}$ | Exothermic ($\text{NaOH}$) or Endothermic ($\text{NH}_4\text{NO}_3$) |
| **Heat of Neutralization (Exp 3B)** | $\text{H}^+(aq) + \text{OH}^-(aq) \rightarrow \text{H}_2\text{O}(l)$ | $\Delta H_{\text{neut}} \approx \mathbf{-57.3\ \text{kJ/mol}}$ (strong acid + strong base) |

### Experiment 4: Second-Order Kinetics — Iodination of Aniline by Optical Density
* Monitored electrophilic aromatic iodination ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NH}_2 + \text{I}_2 \xrightarrow{k_2} p\text{-IC}_6\text{H}_4\text{NH}_2 + \text{HI}$) spectrophotometrically at $460–520\ \text{nm}$. Plotting $\frac{1}{\text{OD}_t}$ vs. time ($t$) yielded a linear slope ($\frac{k_2}{\varepsilon l}$), confirming second-order rate kinetics ($r = k_2[\text{Aniline}][\text{I}_2]$):
  $$\frac{1}{\text{OD}_t} = \frac{1}{\text{OD}_0} + \left(\frac{k_2}{\varepsilon l}\right) t$$

## 3. Bio-Organic Chemistry: Physical Constants, Functional Groups & Food Carbohydrates (CHE 202)

![CHE 202: Bio-Organic Chemistry Laboratory Workflow](CHE202%20-%20Bio-organic%20Chemistry%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Experiments 1 & 2: Melting Point & Organic Solubility Classification
* **Melting Point:** Assessed compound purity via sharp melting range ($\Delta T \le 1–2^\circ\text{C}$) versus impurity-induced melting point depression.
* **Solubility Profiling:** Classified unknown organics sequentially across Distilled Water (polar $\le 5\text{C}$), $5\%\ \text{NaOH}$ (carboxylic acids & phenols), $5\%\ \text{NaHCO}_3$ (carboxylic acids via $\text{CO}_2$ effervescence), $5\%\ \text{HCl}$ (amines), and Conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ (neutral unsaturated/oxygenated compounds).

### Experiments 3 & 4: Functional Group Identification & Food Carbohydrate Detection
* **Functional Groups:** Characterized $-\text{COOH}$ (bicarbonate effervescence / esterification), $\text{Ar}-\text{OH}$ ($1\%\ \text{FeCl}_3$ violet/green complex), $-\text{CHO}/\text{C=O}$ (2,4-DNP, Tollens' silver mirror, Fehling's test), and primary aromatic amines (diazotization + $\beta$-naphthol azo dye).
* **Food Carbohydrates:** Executed **Molisch's test** (general carbohydrates), **Benedict's/Fehling's test** (reducing sugars), **Barfoed's test** (monosaccharide vs. disaccharide kinetics), and **Iodine test** (starch amylose blue-black vs. glycogen red-brown).

## 4. Chemistry of Biomolecules: Distillation, Buffer Formulation, TLC & Food Diagnostics (CHE 203)

![CHE 203: Chemistry of Biomolecules Laboratory](CHE203%20-%20Chemistry%20of%20Biomolecules%20Laboratory.jpg)

### Experiment 1A: Simple Distillation & Carbonyl/Alcohol Differentiation
* Separated miscible volatile liquids ($\Delta\text{bp} > 25^\circ\text{C}$, e.g., acetone $\text{bp } 56^\circ\text{C}$ from water $\text{bp } 100^\circ\text{C}$) and performed **2,4-DNP**, **Iodoform** ($\text{CH}_3\text{C=O}$ methyl ketones), **Schiff's** (aldehydes vs. ketones), and **Lucas tests** ($\text{ZnCl}_2/\text{HCl}$ for $3^\circ, 2^\circ, 1^\circ$ alcohols).

### Experiment 1B: Precision Molar Solution & Molecular Buffer Formulation
* Formulated $1\ \text{M}$ & $1\%\ \text{NaOH}$, $1\ \text{M}$ mineral acids ($\text{HCl}, \text{HNO}_3, \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4, \text{HClO}_4$ via $M_1V_1 = M_2V_2$), $1\ \text{M}$ Tris-HCl ($\text{pH } 8.0$), $0.5\ \text{M}$ EDTA ($\text{pH } 8.0$), **$10\times$ TE Buffer ($\text{pH } 8.0$)**, and **$1\times$ PBS Buffer ($\text{pH } 7.4$)** *(see full recipes in [`06-Instrumentation-and-Buffer-Preparation/`](../06-Instrumentation-and-Buffer-Preparation))*.

### Experiments 3, 4 & 5: Leaf Pigment TLC, Enzymatic Cholesterol & Milk Adulteration
* **Leaf Pigment TLC (Exp 3):** Resolved plant pigments on silica gel (petroleum ether : acetone) by polarity:
  $$\beta\text{-Carotene } (R_f \approx 0.90) > \text{Pheophytin} > \text{Chlorophyll } a \ (R_f \approx 0.45) > \text{Chlorophyll } b \ (R_f \approx 0.35) > \text{Xanthophylls } (R_f \approx 0.25)$$
* **Enzymatic Cholesterol Assay (Exp 4):** Quantified food cholesterol via the CHOD–PAP coupled enzymatic colorimetric method at $500–505\ \text{nm}$.
* **Milk Adulteration Screening (Exp 5):** Tested milk samples for **Starch** ($1\%$ iodine blue-black test), **Soap/Detergent** (alkaline phenolphthalein lather test), and **Formalin** (Hehner's $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4/\text{FeCl}_3$ violet ring test).
