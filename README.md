## 🛠️ Key Hands-On Technical Competencies
* **Nucleic Acid & Cloning Workflows:** Genomic DNA extraction (bacterial, plant CTAB, fungal), total RNA isolation (TRIzol), spectrophotometric purity assessment ($A_{260}/A_{280}$), plasmid DNA extraction, competent cell transformation, PCR amplification, restriction mapping, and agarose gel electrophoresis.
* **Protein & Clinical Enzymology:** Polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE), standard-curve protein quantification, clinical liver enzyme assays (ALT/AST), and enzymatic serum glucose/cholesterol estimation.
* **Immunodiagnostics & Hematology:** Sandwich/indirect ELISA, lateral-flow immunochromatography, latex/bacterial agglutination assays, plasma/serum separation, and peripheral blood cell microscopy.
* **Aseptic & Microbiological Profiling:** Media preparation, aseptic streaking/plating, multi-parameter biochemical characterization of unknown bacterial isolates, and antimicrobial activity testing.
