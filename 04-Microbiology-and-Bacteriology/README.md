## 1. Bacterial Staining Techniques (Simple, Negative, Gram, Endospore & Capsule) (BIO 202)

![BIO 202: Basic Microbiology Laboratory Workflow](BIO202%20-%20Basic%20Microbiology%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Principles & Comparative Summary

| Staining Method | Primary Dye / Reagents | Mordant / Decolorizer | Counterstain | Diagnostic Result & Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (Simple)** | Crystal violet, methylene blue, or safranin (cationic) | None | None | Basic dye chromophores ($+$) bind negatively charged cell walls ($-$), staining cells against a clear background. |
| **Negative Staining** | Nigrosin or India ink (anionic) | None (no heat fixing) | None | Negatively charged dye is repelled by cell surfaces, staining the background dark while leaving cells clear and unshrunken. |
| **Gram Stain (Differential)** | Crystal Violet ($60\ \text{s}$) | Gram's Iodine ($60\ \text{s}$) / 95% Ethanol ($10–15\ \text{s}$) | Safranin ($45–60\ \text{s}$) | **Gram-Positive (Purple):** Thick peptidoglycan retains CV–I complex. **Gram-Negative (Pink/Red):** Thin peptidoglycan + outer lipid membrane dissolves in alcohol, taking up safranin. |
| **Endospore Stain (Schaeffer–Fulton)** | Malachite Green with steam heat ($5\ \text{min}$) | Tap water rinse ($30\ \text{s}$) | Safranin ($60\ \text{s}$) | Heat drives malachite green into keratin-rich spore coats (green endospores); water washes dye from vegetative cells (pink/red). |
| **Capsule Stain (Negative + Positive)** | India Ink / Congo Red (background) | Acid-alcohol (if Congo red) | Crystal Violet / Maneval's stain | Non-ionic polysaccharide capsules repel stains, appearing as a clear halo around stained cells against a dark background. |

### Gram Staining Protocol & Troubleshooting
1. **Smear & Heat Fix:** Emulsify a pinpoint colony in sterile saline, air-dry, and pass through a Bunsen flame $2–3\times$.
2. **Primary Stain & Mordant:** Flood with Crystal Violet ($1\ \text{min}$), rinse $\rightarrow$ flood with Gram's Iodine ($1\ \text{min}$), rinse.
3. **Decolorization (Critical Step):** Tilt slide and apply $95\%$ ethyl alcohol dropwise for $10–15\ \text{sec}$ until runoff runs nearly clear; immediately rinse with water.
4. **Counterstain & Microscopy:** Flood with Safranin ($45–60\ \text{sec}$), rinse, blot dry, and examine under $100\times$ oil immersion.
* **Troubleshooting:** Over-decolorization or old cultures ($>24\ \text{hr}$) cause false Gram-negative pink staining; thick smears or insufficient alcohol ($<5\ \text{sec}$) cause false Gram-positive purple retention.

## 2. Pure Culture Isolation & Viable Enumeration Methods (BIO 202)

### Method Comparison

| Culture Method | Inoculation Technique | Colony Distribution | Primary Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pour Plate (Exp 2)** | Pipette $0.1–1.0\ \text{mL}$ diluted sample into empty sterile Petri dish; pour molten cooled Nutrient Agar ($45–50^\circ\text{C}$) and swirl gently. | Embedded within agar (lenticular) and on surface | Quantitative viable count ($\text{CFU/mL}$) and cultivation of microaerophiles. |
| **Spread Plate (Exp 3)** | Pipette $0.1\ \text{mL}$ diluted sample onto solidified agar; distribute evenly using flame-sterilized L-shaped glass/metal spreader. | Strictly on the agar surface | Quantitative viable count ($\text{CFU/mL}$) of strict aerobes; uniform lawn generation. |
| **Streak Plate (Exp 4)** | Flame-sterilize inoculating loop and streak sequentially across 4 quadrants (flaming loop between quadrants). | Progressively thinned; discrete single colonies in Quadrant 3–4 | Qualitative isolation of pure cultures from mixed clinical/environmental samples. |

### Serial Dilution & Viable Count Calculation ($\text{CFU/mL}$)
1. Prepare ten-fold serial dilutions ($10^{-1}$ through $10^{-6}$) by transferring $1.0\ \text{mL}$ bacterial suspension sequentially into $9.0\ \text{mL}$ sterile physiological saline ($0.85\%\ \text{NaCl}$).
2. Plate $1.0\ \text{mL}$ (Pour Plate) or $0.1\ \text{mL}$ (Spread Plate) from $10^{-4}–10^{-6}$ dilutions, invert plates, and incubate at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $24\ \text{hours}$.
3. Select plates within the statistically valid range of **$30–300\ \text{colonies}$**:
   $$\text{CFU/mL} = \frac{\text{Number of Colonies Counted}}{\text{Dilution Factor} \times \text{Volume Plated (mL)}}$$

## 3. Biochemical Characterization & Presumptive Identification of Unknown Bacteria (BIO 202)

### A. Carbohydrate Fermentation (Glucose, Sucrose, Lactose)
* **Principle:** Phenol red broth tubes containing $0.5–1.0\%$ test carbohydrate and an inverted Durham tube detect acid production (**A**: yellow broth, $\text{pH} < 6.8$), gas production (**G**: bubble trapped in Durham tube, $\text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2$), or peptone deamination (**K**: deep red/magenta alkaline broth, $\text{pH} > 7.4$).

### B. IMViC Series (Differentiation of Enteric Bacilli)

| Test | Medium & Reagent | Positive Result | Negative Result | Target Metabolic Pathway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indole (I)** | Tryptone broth + Kovac's reagent | Cherry-red ring at top layer | Yellow/brown layer | Tryptophanase hydrolyzes tryptophan $\rightarrow$ indole + pyruvate + $\text{NH}_3$. |
| **Methyl Red (M)** | MR-VP broth + Methyl Red indicator | Bright red ($\text{pH} < 4.4$) | Yellow/orange ($\text{pH} > 6.0$) | Stable mixed-acid fermentation of glucose (e.g., *E. coli*). |
| **Voges–Proskauer (Vi)** | MR-VP broth + Barritt's A ($\alpha$-naphthol) & B ($40\%\ \text{KOH}$) | Crimson/pink-red surface layer | Copper/no color change | 2,3-Butanediol fermentation via acetoin intermediate (e.g., *Klebsiella*). |
| **Citrate (C)** | Simmon's Citrate agar slant (bromothymol blue) | Prussian blue slant + growth | Unchanged forest green slant | Citrate permease & lyase utilize citrate as sole carbon source. |

### C. Oxygen Utilization, Nitrate Reduction & Amino Acid/Protein Hydrolysis

| Biochemical Test | Substrate & Reagent | Positive Reaction | Negative Reaction | Physiological Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Catalase** | $3\%\ \text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ on slide/colony | Immediate effervescence ($\text{O}_2$ bubbles) | No bubbles | Differentiates *Staphylococcus* ($+$) from *Streptococcus* ($-$). |
| **Oxidase** | Kovac's oxidase reagent (TMPD) | Deep purple/blue within $10–30\ \text{sec}$ | Colorless / unchanged | Detects cytochrome $c$ oxidase (*Pseudomonas* $+$, *Enterobacteriaceae* $-$). |
| **Nitrate Reduction** | Nitrate broth + Sulfanilic acid & $\alpha$-naphthylamine (+ Zn dust) | Red before Zn ($\text{NO}_3^- \rightarrow \text{NO}_2^-$) OR colorless after Zn ($\text{N}_2$ gas) | Turns red only after adding Zinc dust | Anaerobic respiration using nitrate as terminal electron acceptor. |
| **Urease** | Christensen's Urea agar (phenol red) | Hot pink / magenta throughout slant | Yellow/orange slant | Urease hydrolyzes urea $\rightarrow 2\text{NH}_3 + \text{CO}_2$ (rapidly $+$ in *Proteus* sp.). |
| **Gelatin Hydrolysis** | Nutrient gelatin deep ($22^\circ\text{C}$, $24–48\ \text{h}$, chill on ice $30\ \text{min}$) | Medium remains liquid after chilling | Medium solidifies on ice | Extracellular gelatinase cleaves gelatin into soluble peptides. |
