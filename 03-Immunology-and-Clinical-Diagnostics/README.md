## 1. Peripheral Blood Film (PBF) Examination & ABO/Rh Blood Grouping (BBT 316)

![BBT 316: Immunology Laboratory Workflow](BBT316%20-%20Immunology%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Part A: Identification of Blood Cells & Differential Leukocyte Count (Exp 1)
* **Principle:** Romanowsky stains (Leishman / Wright–Giemsa) utilize methylene blue (stains acidic nuclear DNA/RNA blue-purple) and eosin (stains basic hemoglobin and eosinophilic granules red-orange) to differentiate leukocytes across the monolayer feathered edge under $100\times$ oil-immersion microscopy.
* **Protocol Summary:** Spread $2–3\ \mu\text{L}$ EDTA blood at $30^\circ–45^\circ$ $\rightarrow$ air-dry $\rightarrow$ fix with Leishman stain ($2\ \text{min}$) $\rightarrow$ dilute $1:1$ with buffered water ($\text{pH } 6.8$, $8–10\ \text{min}$) $\rightarrow$ rinse, dry, and perform a $100\text{-cell}$ differential leukocyte count (DLC).

| Cell Type | Size & Nuclear Morphology | Cytoplasmic Features | Normal Adult DLC |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neutrophil** | $12–15\ \mu\text{m}$; multilobed ($2–5$ lobes) connected by thin strands | Pale pink with fine lilac/neutral granules | **$40 – 70\%$** |
| **Lymphocyte** | $7–12\ \mu\text{m}$; large, round, dense dark-purple nucleus | Scant sky-blue rim without granules | **$20 – 40\%$** |
| **Monocyte** | $15–20\ \mu\text{m}$ (largest); kidney/horseshoe-shaped chromatin | Abundant slate-gray/blue "ground-glass" cytoplasm | **$2 – 8\%$** |
| **Eosinophil** | $12–15\ \mu\text{m}$; bilobed ("spectacle-shaped") nucleus | Coarse, bright red-orange refractile granules | **$1 – 4\%$** |
| **Basophil** | $12–15\ \mu\text{m}$; S-shaped nucleus obscured by granules | Dense, dark blue-black metachromatic granules | **$0 – 1\%$** |

### Part B: ABO and Rh(D) Blood Grouping by Direct Haemagglutination (Exp 2)

| Anti-A Serum | Anti-B Serum | Anti-D Serum | Blood Group | Serum Isoagglutinins |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Agglutination (+) | No Clumping (–) | Agglutination (+) | **A Positive ($\text{A}^+$)** | Anti-B |
| No Clumping (–) | Agglutination (+) | Agglutination (+) | **B Positive ($\text{B}^+$)** | Anti-A |
| Agglutination (+) | Agglutination (+) | No Clumping (–) | **AB Negative ($\text{AB}^-$)** | None |
| No Clumping (–) | No Clumping (–) | No Clumping (–) | **O Negative ($\text{O}^-$)** | Anti-A & Anti-B |

## 2. Diagnostic Agglutination Assays: VDRL, Latex CRP & Widal Test (BBT 316)

| Assay | Antigen / Reagent | Target in Patient Serum | Clinical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VDRL Test (Exp 3)** | Cardiolipin–lecithin–cholesterol suspension | Non-treponemal reagin antibodies ($\text{IgM}/\text{IgG}$) | Screening for syphilis (*Treponema pallidum*) |
| **CRP Latex (Exp 4)** | Polystyrene latex coated with anti-human CRP $\text{IgG}$ | C-Reactive Protein ($\ge 6\ \text{mg/L}$) | Acute-phase inflammation & tissue injury biomarker |
| **Widal Test (Exp 5)** | Stained killed *Salmonella* somatic (O) & flagellar (H, AH, BH) antigens | Anti-*S. typhi* & anti-*S. paratyphi* A/B agglutinins | Serodiagnosis of enteric (typhoid/paratyphoid) fever |

### Protocol Highlights & Semiquantitative Calculations
* **VDRL Slide Flocculation (Exp 3):** Heat-inactivate serum at $56^\circ\text{C}$ for $30\ \text{min}$ $\rightarrow$ mix $50\ \mu\text{L}$ serum with $17\ \mu\text{L}$ antigen suspension $\rightarrow$ rotate at $180\ \text{rpm}$ for $4\ \text{min}$ and inspect at $10\times$ magnification.
* **CRP Latex Titration (Exp 4):** Positive qualitative agglutination indicates $\text{CRP} \ge 6\ \text{mg/L}$. Serial two-fold saline dilutions ($1:2$ to $1:32$) determine approximate serum levels:
  $$\text{Approximate CRP (mg/L)} = 6\ \text{mg/L} \times \text{Highest Positive Dilution Titer}$$
* **Widal Titration (Exp 5):** Tested serum volumes of $80, 40, 20, 10,$ and $5\ \mu\text{L}$ (corresponding to titers $1:20$ to $1:320$) against O, H, AH, and BH antigens (titers $\ge 1:160$ or a four-fold rise indicate active infection in endemic regions).

## 3. HBsAg Detection via Sandwich ELISA & Immunochromatography (BBT 316)

### Part A: Sandwich ELISA for HBsAg (Exp 6)
* **Principle:** Microtiter wells pre-coated with monoclonal anti-HBs capture antibodies bind serum HBsAg, which is subsequently bound by HRP-conjugated anti-HBs detection antibodies ($\text{Ab–Ag–Ab–HRP}$ sandwich). TMB substrate oxidation generates a blue color converted to yellow upon $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ addition, measured at $450\ \text{nm}$.
* **Workflow:** Incubate $50–100\ \mu\text{L}$ controls/serum + $50\ \mu\text{L}$ HRP-conjugate ($37^\circ\text{C}$, $60\ \text{min}$) $\rightarrow$ wash $5\times$ ($300\ \mu\text{L}$ buffer/well) $\rightarrow$ incubate with TMB Chromogen A + Substrate B ($37^\circ\text{C}$, $15\ \text{min}$, dark) $\rightarrow$ stop with $50\ \mu\text{L}$ $1\ \text{M H}_2\text{SO}_4$ and read $\text{OD}_{450}$ (ref $620\ \text{nm}$).
* **Cut-Off Calculation:**
  $$\text{Cut-Off Value (COV)} = \text{Mean OD}_{\text{Negative Control}} + 0.05 \quad (\text{Reactive: } \text{OD}_{\text{Sample}} \ge \text{COV})$$

### Part B: Rapid Immunochromatographic Test (ICT) for HBsAg (Exp 7)
* **Principle & Interpretation:** Lateral-flow capillary migration forms an HBsAg–colloidal gold anti-HBs complex captured at the Test line (T), while unbound conjugate binds goat anti-mouse $\text{IgG}$ at the Control line (C). Two bands (`C + T`) = Positive; one band (`C` only) = Negative; no `C` band = Invalid.

## 4. Foundational Cell Biology, Innate Antimicrobials & Clinical Assays (BIO 103)

![BIO 103 Lab: Biology – 1 Laboratory Workflow](BIO103%20-%20Biology-1%20Laboratory%20Workflow.jpg)

### Experiment 1: Potentiometric pH Analysis of Food & Beverages
Calibrated glass-electrode pH meter ($\text{pH } 4.0, 7.0, 10.0$ buffers) to classify samples:

| Sample Type | Expected pH Range | Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lemon / Citrus Juice** | $2.0 – 3.0$ | Strongly Acidic |
| **Carbonated Soda / Vinegar** | $2.5 – 3.5$ | Acidic |
| **Fresh Milk** | $6.5 – 6.7$ | Slightly Acidic |
| **Distilled Water** | $6.8 – 7.0$ | Neutral |
| **Baking Soda Solution / Egg White** | $7.8 – 8.5$ | Alkaline / Basic |

### Experiment 2: Eukaryotic Microscopy & Innate Lysozyme Antimicrobial Assay
* **Light Microscopy:** Identified plasma membrane, cytoplasm, and nucleus in methylene-blue-stained human buccal epithelial cells and cell wall/central vacuole in iodine-stained onion epidermal cells ($10\times$ and $40\times$ objectives).
* **Saliva & Tears Lysozyme Assay:** Evaluated innate lysozyme hydrolysis of bacterial peptidoglycan $\beta\text{-}(1,4)$-glycosidic bonds via Kirby–Bauer-style disc diffusion on *Micrococcus luteus* / *Bacillus* sp. Nutrient Agar lawns ($37^\circ\text{C}$, $24\ \text{hr}$), measuring clear zones of inhibition against antibiotic positive and sterile saline negative controls.

### Experiments 3 & 4: Slide Blood Grouping & Enzymatic Serum Glucose
* Executed direct ABO/Rh haemagglutination typing and quantitative serum glucose measurement via the GOD–POD Trinder colorimetric assay at $505\ \text{nm}$ (normal fasting range: $70–99\ \text{mg/dL}$).
